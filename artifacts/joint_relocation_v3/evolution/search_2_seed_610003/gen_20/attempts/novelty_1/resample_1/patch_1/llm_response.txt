<DESCRIPTION>
Test relocating three particles instead of four when relative fitness loss is at most 0.075 and swarm diameter exceeds 2.5 default radii. A dispersed swarm with limited observed deterioration may benefit from retaining two ordinary PSO moves. Preserve the existing radius rule to isolate the allocation change. The supplied constant-three policy had worse mean error but lower error on case_015; differing radius policies prevent attributing that tradeoff to count alone. This conditional allocation is an untested hypothesis.
