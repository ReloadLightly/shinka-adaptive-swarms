<DESCRIPTION>
Test whether preserving some particles while widening relocation after substantial fitness deterioration improves recovery. Small observed drops relocate 40% of particles locally; moderate drops relocate 60% farther; large drops relocate 80% at 2.5 times the baseline radius. For small or moderate drops, an already dispersed swarm relocates only 40%, preserving existing exploration.
Retain velocities and reevaluate memories: memory refresh consumed only 0.32–0.65% of evaluations, so the supplied feedback gives little reason to sacrifice useful memories for that saving. Persistent interval-end errors motivate testing broader recovery, but do not establish that insufficient relocation caused those errors. These thresholds are reusable hypotheses requiring evaluation, not demonstrated improvements.
