<DESCRIPTION>
Keep radius_scale at 1.5 and default to four particles, which achieved lower measured mean error than three (3.81 versus 3.95). Relocate only three when relative fitness deterioration is at most 5% and swarm diameter exceeds three default radii. This tests whether preserving two ordinary PSO moves helps a dispersed swarm after limited deterioration. Aggregate feedback does not establish this mechanism; the conditional policy requires evaluation.
