This supports testing stronger particle preservation, although it does not establish causality.
Cap relocation at 60% to preserve two spatial anchors. As a new refinement, relocate only one of five particles when deterioration is mild and recent improvement indicates productive search. Moderate changes still receive two exploratory particles during productive search. Preserve memory reevaluation, velocities, and the existing radius adaptation. The hypothesis is that retaining useful search structure improves recovery while a small exploratory contingent supplies diversity. This proposed refinement requires evaluation.
