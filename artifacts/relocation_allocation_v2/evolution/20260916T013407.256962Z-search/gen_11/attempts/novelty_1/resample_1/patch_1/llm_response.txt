<DESCRIPTION>
Restrict reduced allocation to the larger-radius regime, but allow two relocations when recent improvement covers at least half the observed loss. This tests whether partial recovery supports preserving more ordinary PSO motion.
The previous radius-gated policy achieved mean error 3.26 versus 3.36 currently. In case_006, it reduced error from 5.224 to 3.169 and interval-end error from 4.313 to 2.212. However, larger-radius cases executed count two in only about 7–14% of responses, and case_009 retained interval-end error 3.401. Broadening that branch tests a competing recovery hypothesis; these diagnostics do not establish that fewer relocations will improve performance. The proposal requires evaluation.
