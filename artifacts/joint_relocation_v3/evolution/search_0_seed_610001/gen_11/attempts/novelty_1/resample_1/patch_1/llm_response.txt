<DESCRIPTION>
Test constant count three with radius multiplier 1.25. Three-particle relocation at 1.5 had lower worst-case error and dispersion, while four-particle relocation at 1.25 achieved the best supplied mean error. Combining these settings tests whether a smaller radius improves three-particle tracking while preserving ordinary PSO motion for two particles. This combination is untested; the existing results do not establish that its effects will combine favorably.
