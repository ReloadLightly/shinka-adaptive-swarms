Use four particles at radius multiplier 1.25, which achieved lower measured mean error (3.76) than the current policy (3.95). Expand to 1.5 only when relative fitness loss exceeds 10% and recent improvement is less than one quarter of the fitness drop. This tests whether selective expansion helps recovery while avoiding the consistently larger radii of the displacement-based policy. Its performance remains to be measured.
