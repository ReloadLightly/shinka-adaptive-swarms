<DESCRIPTION>
Keep count four and use radius multiplier 1.5, increasing it to 1.75 only when relative fitness deterioration exceeds 0.1 and swarm diameter is at most three default radii. The constant-1.5 policy matched the current policy’s rounded mean error and had a lower worst-case error. This motivates testing whether modest expansion during deterioration helps compact swarms recover. The trigger is an untested hypothesis; the supplied diagnostics do not establish that insufficient coverage caused the errors.
