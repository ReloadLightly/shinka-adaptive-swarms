# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns
- **The current best, G28 “Spread-Threshold Relocation with Fixed Radius,” supersedes G11’s lead.** G28 and G27 achieve score **0.214451**, mean error **3.663062**, using three particles when normalized diameter exceeds **4.5**, otherwise four, at radius multiplier **1.25**. Compared with G11/G25’s threshold four (**0.214212 / 3.668275**), mean error falls **0.142%**: cases_002 and _003 improve, while the other 14 case errors remain identical.
- **Predominantly four-particle relocation with occasional three-particle responses remains the strongest observed combination.** G28 reduces mean error **2.54%** versus fixed-four G9 (**0.210153 / 3.758438**) and **11.94%** versus corrected baseline G0 (**0.193814 / 4.159579**) on this search suite. Fixed-three G14 and full-swarm G5 at multiplier 1.25 also trail it.
- **G26’s displacement-gated threshold ties the simpler current best.** Its threshold `4 + 0.5*d/(1+d)` produces the same saved case metrics as G27/G28, including score **0.214451**. This supports its observed performance, but provides no evidence that adding displacement improves upon the fixed threshold 4.5.
## Ineffective Approaches
- **G29’s continuous radius taper worsens both mean and tail performance.** Its fixed-four allocation and spread-dependent multiplier yield **0.208591 / 3.794062**, approximately **3.58% higher mean error** than G28. Worst-case error rises from **9.4230 to 12.1197**, and case_015 interval-end error rises from **7.2366 to 10.8351**. Against fixed-four G9, which isolates the radius-policy change more directly, G29 also increases mean error by **0.95%**.
- **Other tested radius changes also underperform the fixed 1.25 multiplier.** Earlier G19’s reduction to 1.125 scored **0.197054**, while G22’s increase to 1.3125 in the four-particle branch scored **0.209616**. Together with G29, these results favor the tested fixed-radius combination; they do not establish why the alternatives lose.
- **Neither larger diameter thresholds nor fitness-loss conditioning consistently helps.** Threshold-five G20 (**0.213874**) and threshold-six G23/G24 (**0.210023**) trail G28; threshold-eight G12 (**0.212042**) outperforms threshold six, preserving the earlier nonmonotonic pattern. G21’s deterioration switch remains substantially weaker at **0.196264 / 4.095171**.
## Implementation Insights
- **The current best uses a compact, stateless decision rule.** It divides diameter by `max(default_radius, 1e-12)`, applies strict `> 4.5`, clamps the integer count to swarm size, and returns **1.25**. Four-particle responses account for **2,952/3,437 (85.89%)**, versus **85.78%** for G11. Similar aggregate allocation frequencies therefore conceal meaningful differences in response timing and trajectories.
- **Equivalent results must not be counted as independent discoveries.** G25 reproduces G11’s threshold-four results. G27 and G28 express the same mathematical threshold using multiplication and division and have identical saved case metrics. G26 also matches those metrics, although its displacement-dependent rule is not mathematically identical.
- **Adapter and simulator corrections remain shared controls.** Counts three and four allocate **60% and 80%** of a five-particle swarm; encoding fractions **0.5 and 0.7** only implement integer selection. Requested, allocated and queried counts match without horizon truncation. Memory reevaluation and retained velocities are not evolutionary gains, and particles excluded from relocation still undergo ordinary PSO motion.
## Performance Analysis
- **The updated leading order is G26 = G27 = G28 > G11 = G25 > G20 > G18 > G12 > G9.** Two-decimal scores conceal these distinctions. G28 improves mean error over G11 by just **0.005213**, corresponding to a score gain of **0.000239**. Because score is `1/(1+mean error)`, this ranking reflects mean performance alone.
- **The advantage over fixed-four G9 remains concentrated in particular cases.** G28 improves seven cases and worsens nine. Its case_015 reduction of **2.7271** exceeds its summed reduction across all 16 cases (**1.5260**). Excluding case_015, G28 averages **3.2791**, versus G9’s **3.1990**. However, G28’s incremental improvement over G11 comes entirely from cases_002 and _003, not case_015.
