<DESCRIPTION>
Test a diameter threshold of 4 + 0.5*d/(1+d), where d is observed best displacement normalized by default radius. Fixed thresholds four and five achieved lower search error than six; this proposal stays near four while modestly favoring four relocated particles when observed displacement is larger. Keep radius_scale at 1.25 to isolate the allocation change. This is an untested hypothesis, not an established improvement.
</DESCRIPTION>
