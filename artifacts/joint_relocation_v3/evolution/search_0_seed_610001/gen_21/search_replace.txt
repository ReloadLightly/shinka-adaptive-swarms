<DESCRIPTION>
Keep allocation at four particles and vary radius slightly around 1.1875, whose measured mean error was marginally lower than 1.25. Normalize swarm diameter by default_radius, then bound the multiplier to [1.15625, 1.21875]. This tests whether compact swarms benefit from tighter relocation and dispersed swarms from broader relocation. The recovery-based policy showed no clear aggregate improvement; spread provides a different public signal. Performance gains remain untested.
