Restrict the existing recovery-credit rule to larger relocation radii. At severity 1, the adaptive rule increased mean error from 3.1216 to 3.3128; at severity 3, it decreased mean error from 3.6068 to 3.4065. Executed count-two responses were also more frequent at severity 1. Recovery diagnostics show mixed effects: case_006 interval-end error rose from 2.21217 to 4.31346, while case_010 improved from 2.93968 to 2.00686.
Use the public default_radius to retain constant-three allocation in the smaller-radius regime and preserve the current adaptive rule in the larger-radius regime. The threshold separates the supplied regimes. This tests a search-supported interaction between relocation scale and allocation; improved performance and generalization remain unverified.
