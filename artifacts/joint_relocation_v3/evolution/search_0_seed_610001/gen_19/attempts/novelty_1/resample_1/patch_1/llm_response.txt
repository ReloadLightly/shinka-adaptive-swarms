<DESCRIPTION>
Test count three when relative fitness deterioration is at most 0.025, otherwise count four, with radius_scale fixed at 1.25. This preserves two ordinary PSO movers after mild losses while retaining broader relocation after larger losses. The current radius adjustment produced mixed per-case changes without improving the reported mean; fixing the radius makes comparison against the measured count-four/radius-1.25 policy easier to interpret. The threshold is a hypothesis requiring evaluation, not an established improvement.
</DESCRIPTION>
