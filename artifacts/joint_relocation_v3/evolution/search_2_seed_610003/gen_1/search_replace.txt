<DESCRIPTION>
Test relocating four particles at twice the baseline radius, leaving one particle to continue ordinary PSO motion. In five dimensions, uniform-volume samples concentrate toward the relocation boundary; doubling the radius increases coverage around the refreshed best while retaining one existing search position.
The baseline’s large interval-end errors motivate testing broader relocation, although they do not establish its cause. This constant pair tests the joint allocation–radius tradeoff without interpreting fitness loss as geometric displacement. Improvement remains a hypothesis for the sixteen-case evaluation.
</DESCRIPTION>
