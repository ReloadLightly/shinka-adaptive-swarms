Three particles at radius `1.0` delivered competitive mean error and better worst-case performance; this mutation tests whether moderate expansion also benefits that allocation.

This preserves the winning response for compact swarms while testing whether already dispersed swarms benefit from more ordinary PSO motion; spread’s predictive value remains unproven.
